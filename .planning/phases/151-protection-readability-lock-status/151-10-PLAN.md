<objective>
Measure what the firmware actually grew, on a cold build of all three AVR targets, then fund it
with new named, SHA-attributed exemptions on both axes and re-arm every tripwire at the new
floor.

Purpose: D-01's stated cost, and it is the tightest constraint in the phase. Measured from the
committed baselines: leonardo's MERGE-05 flash headroom is **0 B** — BASE-01 `flash_used` 26906
plus an allowance of exactly `0 + 96 + 210 = 306` equals the live 27212. RAM headroom is **0 B**
on all three targets: BASE-01 `ram_used` 1573/1579/2014 plus a 2 B tolerance equals the live
1575/1581/2016. So **any** new flash byte on any target needs a third named flash exemption, and
**any** new RAM byte on any target needs a second named RAM exemption — and D-01's cost
paragraph never mentions RAM at all. A single `uint8_t` on `firestarter_handle_t` costs 1 B and
trips it; AVR aligns every scalar to 1 byte, so no padding absorbs it.

Two figures must not be conflated. Leonardo's **physical** free flash is 5556 B, materially
larger since quick task `260820-a7w` licensed the reported ceiling to the MCU's real 32768 B by
forfeiting Caterina's 4096 B. Leonardo's **MERGE-05** flash headroom is 0 B. That task moved the
first and deliberately left the second alone; the baseline's own note says so.

Output: a cold-measurement transcript, two new exemptions, a re-recorded live baseline, and a
severed `*_v151*` tripwire family.

**Requirement flips owned by this plan: none.** It advances LOCK-02.
</objective>
