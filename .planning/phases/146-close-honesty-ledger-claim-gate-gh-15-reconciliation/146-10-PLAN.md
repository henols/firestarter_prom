<objective>
Draft both release bodies — firmware and host — version-agnostic, matched to the ledger's permitted wording, and
written for a stranger rather than for someone who has read this milestone's records.

Purpose: CLOSE-05 asks that release notes describe the programming-behaviour change and the per-run pulse
override in terms a stranger can act on. It does **not** ask for a cut, a tag or a push, and D-01 keeps all
three out of this phase — the merge, the CI-triggered cut, any package-index dispatch and the version tag belong
to the milestone-close command. D-02 makes both bodies version-agnostic because both repositories' CI derives
the next version from a tag scan at push time, so any version written here could be invalidated by a stray CI
fire between now and the cut. Two mechanical traps apply: two of the donor bodies' section headings contain the
bare claim word this phase's own gate forbids, so those headings must be renamed rather than copied; and both
bodies are caveat-required targets, so each must carry the ceiling.

Output: `146-RELEASE-NOTES-fw.md` and `146-RELEASE-NOTES-app.md`, both green under the phase's own claim gate,
both behind the blocking operator wording review in plan 146-12.
</objective>
