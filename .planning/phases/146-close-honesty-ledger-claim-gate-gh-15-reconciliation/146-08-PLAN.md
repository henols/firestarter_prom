<objective>
Write `146-LEDGER.md`: the milestone's honesty ledger, pairing every permitted claim with its explicit non-claim,
leading with the program-VCC ceiling and the asymmetric bench coverage.

Purpose: this is the single source of the permitted wording, and every artifact written after it — both release
bodies and the public reconciliation — must match it. That is why constraint 1 puts it first. Its raw material is
already written, hashed and committed across five phase records; the ledger **cites** rather than re-measures.
Two things make it delicate. The gate this phase built forbids twelve claim shapes with no proximity window and
no exclusion mechanism, and the bench record the ledger must cite contains one of those literals in its own
boundary sentence — so citation discipline is a mechanical requirement, not a stylistic preference. And three
records disagree about how many carry-forwards there are; the ledger's job is to state both readings and let the
authoritative table settle it, not to pick the number that reads best.

Output: `146-LEDGER.md`, green under the phase's own claim gate.
</objective>
