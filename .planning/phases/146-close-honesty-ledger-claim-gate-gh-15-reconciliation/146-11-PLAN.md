<objective>
Discharge CLOSE-01's second claim: arm the gate against the real files and record it being seen to fail on a
planted violation in one of them, then bring every standing gate in the phase green in a single pass.

Purpose: CLOSE-01 makes two claims that look like one. *Armed against the real files* is about the default target
list; *seen to fail on a planted violation* is about the pattern table. A fixture suite proves the second and says
nothing about the first — a checker with perfect fixtures and a stale default list is exactly the recorded defect
this shape exists to catch. So D-12 requires both proofs, and this plan owns the one the suite cannot give: a
plant into a **real, tracked, committed** closing artifact, observed through the no-argument no-environment path,
then reverted to byte identity. Two measured cautions apply: an exit status taken after a pipe reports the pipe's
last command, which in a recorded case printed a zero for a script that had just failed; and the revert is proven
by blob identity, not by reading the file.

Output: `146-CITATIONS.md` §4, and every gate in the phase green in one recorded pass.
</objective>
