## 3. Existing Detect-HW Scheme (Anders R41-on-A3)

Per `.planning/v1.7/notes/CHAT-INTEL.md` §1 and the per-rev `.kicad_sch` mine (`.planning/phases/31-upstream-shield-archaeology/mine-notes.md` §Per-rev R41), Anders introduced a version-detect resistor divider at JP4 / R41 feeding Arduino ADC pin A3 starting in the early Rev2 era (commit `a252e39`, 2024-10-08). Rev2.1 carries R41 = 4k7; Rev2.2 uses the same schematic blob as Rev2.1 (R41 = 4k7); Rev2.3 changed R41 to 10k (commit `c2bd111`, 2025-06-24). A discrepancy exists between Anders's CHAT-INTEL statement ("10k version resistor for Rev 2.2", 2025-04-28) and the schematic evidence: the committed schematic for Rev2.2 shows R41 = 4k7. This §3 table captures the per-rev ground truth that Phase 34 firmware ADC-detect plumbing will consume.
