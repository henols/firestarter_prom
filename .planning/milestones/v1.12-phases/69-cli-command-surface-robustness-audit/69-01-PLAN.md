<objective>
Fix the live `TypeError: '<=' not supported between instances of 'list' and 'int'`
crash in `ic_layout._generate_pin_names_for_display` at its root, and pin the fixed
behavior with a new unit test file.

`pinouts.json` stores `vpp-pin`/`oe-pin`/`rw-pin` as single-element lists (e.g. `[22]`)
and is CORRECT — it must NOT change. The display layer must extract the scalar before
every comparison and index, mirroring the existing analog `database.get_bus_config`
(inline `val[0] if isinstance(val, list) else val`).

Purpose: Closes SC#1 (root fix, not one call site) and contributes the unit-level half
of SC#3 (regression tests pin the crash class).
Output: Patched `ic_layout.py`; new `tests/test_ic_layout.py`.
</objective>
