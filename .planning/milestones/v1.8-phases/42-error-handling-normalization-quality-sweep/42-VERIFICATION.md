Logged as concern because future read-path-bisect work (v1.9 RCA) will see this as a non-trivial change to the ring-fenced body. Recommend documenting the rename in the GATE-1.8d acceptance log so v1.9 RCA does not flag it later.

The 0.12% headroom is real but slim; future test removals or refactors will easily dip below 70% and break CI. Recommend a small future test top-up (`test_logging_utils.py` or `test_utils.py` extensions) to lift headroom.
